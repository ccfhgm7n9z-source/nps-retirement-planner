How the calculator works

  • Starting corpus is the current market value as of the valuation date.
  • Future monthly contributions begin in the selected first contribution month.
  • Contributions are assumed at month-end.
  • The monthly contribution increases by the selected step-up each year in the selected month.
  • Annual returns are treated as effective annual rates and converted to equivalent monthly rates.
  • The projection stops at the month before the retirement month.
  • Inflation-adjusted value discounts the retirement corpus back to the valuation date.

Important limitations

  • Returns are assumptions, not guarantees or forecasts.
  • Asset allocation is displayed but no separate asset-class return forecasts are made.
  • Charges, taxes, exit rules, annuity rates and future regulatory changes are not modelled.
  • Monthly contribution growth may become unrealistic over a 35-year horizon; review affordability and salary growth.
  • For retirement decisions, verify current NPS/PFRDA rules and consult a qualified adviser.

Privacy & data

This is a static, local-first web app. Your inputs and saved scenarios stay in your browser's local storage. The app does not send financial information to a server. Clearing browser site data will remove locally saved scenarios. If you use a shared device, avoid saving sensitive details.
